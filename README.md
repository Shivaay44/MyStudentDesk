## 🌟 Included Tools & Capabilities

### 1. 🇮🇳 India Exams & Boards
- **JEE Rank Predictor**: 0–300 raw marks to NTA Percentile, Expected AIR (~14.5 Lakh candidate pool), Category rank, and JoSAA NIT/IIIT college branch admission chance matrix.
- **NEET UG Score & Rank Calculator**: Fast answer key scoring tally (+4 for correct, -1 for wrong, 0 for unattempted) for Physics, Chemistry, Biology with Expected AIR and AIQ 15% / State 85% Government Medical seat probability.
- **CBSE Percentage Calculator**: Class 10 & Class 12 (Science PCM/PCB, Commerce, Arts) streams with official **Best 5 rule** and vocational subject substitution.
- **CBSE CGPA Calculator**: A1 to E2 Grade points entry with standard **9.5 multiplier** percentage conversion.
- **CUET Score Calculator**: Combined score aggregator for Delhi University (DU CSAS 800/600 scale) and BHU target programs.
- **College Merit & Cutoff Calculator**: 12th Board % + Entrance Exam % weighted composite index and Tamil Nadu Engineering (TNEA 200 cutoff).

### 2. 🎓 Academic & Grading
- **Percentage Calculator**: 4 modes (Marks to %, What is X% of Y, Percentage Change / Growth %, Discount Price).
- **CGPA → Percentage Converter**: Preset formulas (CBSE 9.5x, Mumbai University, VTU 10x - 7.5, Anna University 10.0x, KTU, custom multiplier).
- **GPA & SGPA Multi-Semester Calculator**: Multi-semester credit-hour weighted SGPA & Cumulative CGPA calculator.
- **Marks Calculator & Target Analyzer**: Multi-subject entry, aggregate marks, passing margin, and subject breakdown.

### 3. ⏱️ Attendance & Time Management
- **Attendance & Bunk Calculator**: Attended vs total classes, **Live Bunk Meter** (safe bunks left before dropping below 75%/80%), and shortage warning indicator.
- **Required Attendance Calculator**: Attendance recovery seeker — computes exact consecutive classes needed to restore attendance back to 75%, 80%, or 85%.

### 4. 🧮 Math & Science Suite
- **Scientific Calculator**: Comprehensive functions ($\sin, \cos, \tan, \ln, \log, \sqrt{x}, x^y, n!$), Radian/Degree mode, interactive calculation tape.
- **Linear Equation Solver**: 2-Variable ($2\times 2$) and 3-Variable ($3\times 3$) system solver with step-by-step Cramer's Rule determinants.
- **Quadratic Equation Solver**: $ax^2 + bx + c = 0$ solver with discriminant $D$, real/complex conjugate roots, vertex coordinates, and factored form.
- **Matrix Calculator**: Addition, Subtraction, Multiplication, Determinant ($|A|$), Transpose ($A^T$), and Inverses ($A^{-1}$) for $2\times 2$ and $3\times 3$.
- **Permutation & Combination ($nPr, nCr$)**: Step-by-step factorial expansions and with/without repetition rules.
- **Probability Calculator & Simulator**: Single/compound events, Bayes theorem, live Monte Carlo **Coin Flip simulator**.
- **Comprehensive Unit Converter**: 80+ units across Length, Mass, Temperature, Data Storage, Speed, Time, and Indian Area units (Bigha, Guntha, Quintal).

### 5. 🛠️ Productivity, Writing & Document Tools
- **Pomodoro & Focus Timer**: 25/5 & 50/10 intervals, task checklist, streak tracker, and **Synthesized Ambient Audio** (Rain Brown Noise, Pure White Noise, 432Hz Study Drone generated natively in-browser).
- **Study Stopwatch & Lap Tracker**: Subject tags (Physics, Chemistry, Maths, Bio, Coding) with session stopwatch and split lap logger.
- **Age & Milestone Calculator**: Exact age (Years, Months, Days, Hours), next birthday countdown, zodiac sign, lifetime heartbeats counter.
- **Date Difference & Working Days**: Total days, business working days (Mon-Fri / Mon-Sat) and exam prep duration.
- **Student BMI & Health Calculator**: BMI gauge meter, ideal healthy weight range for height, daily water requirement guide.
- **Word & Character Counter**: Real-time words, characters, reading/speaking time, case converters, keyword density analyzer.
- **Citation Generator**: APA 7th, MLA 9th, Chicago, Harvard, IEEE citations for Books, Journals, Websites, and YouTube videos.
- **QR Code Generator**: Custom colored high-res QR codes for study links, Wi-Fi credentials, notes with instant PNG download.
- **Client-Side PDF Tools (100% Private)**:
  - **Merge PDFs**: Combine multiple assignment sheets/PDFs.
  - **Split PDF**: Extract specific page ranges.
  - **Images to PDF**: Convert photos of handwritten notes into a clean multi-page A4 PDF.

## ⚡ Quick Start / Local Setup

```bash
# Clone the repository
git clone https://github.com/Shivaay44/MyStudentDesk.git

# Navigate to project directory
cd MyStudentDesk

# Install dependencies
npm install

# Start local development server
npm run dev
```

Open [http://localhost:5173](http://localhost:5173) in your browser.

---

## 🔒 Privacy & Security
All computations, PDF merges/splits, and image conversions occur **100% client-side** in your local browser memory. No student data, marksheets, or assignments are uploaded to any external server.
